06 — FAQ

Quick answers

The questions tree care companies and contractors ask us most often before choosing a stump removal attachment.

What is the difference between a stump crown and a stump auger?+

A crown cuts the soil around the stump and pulls it out whole; an auger grinds it into chips directly in the ground. Choose a crown when the stump must be removed, an auger when you want the ground ready straight away.

Which sizes are available?+

Crowns of 35, 40, 45, 50, 55 and 60 cm in diameter (plus optional wings); augers from a minimum of 50 cm to a maximum of 150 cm wide.

Right- or left-hand rotation: which do I need?+

It depends on the direction of rotation of the hydraulic motor fitted to your excavator. We build both versions: tell us your machine and we will tell you which one to choose.

Do you ship outside Italy?+

Yes. We supply customers across Italy and abroad directly from our workshop in Villanova sull'Arda (Piacenza, Italy).
